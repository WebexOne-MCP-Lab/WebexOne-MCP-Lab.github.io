# Meeting Follow-Up

Turn an authorized completed meeting transcript or summary into a reviewed follow-up package.

Resolve the meeting, retrieve the transcript or summary via the Webex Meetings MCP server, extract decisions and action items, produce a draft, obtain approval, then post the recap and return confirmation.

Mark missing owners or dates as `unassigned` / `not specified`. Load translation and attendance skills only when requested.

For the full step-by-step workflow, see [references/workflow.md](references/workflow.md).
For default output sections and formatting, see [references/output-format.md](references/output-format.md).

## Customization

Customers may change section names, ordering, space naming, and action-item labels. Do not remove evidence, approval, or data-gap fields.
