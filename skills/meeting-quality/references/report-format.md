# Meeting Quality — Default Report Sections

- **Meeting reference** — title, ID, date, and duration
- **Incident summary** — concise overview of the quality issue
- **Observations and evidence** — metrics with source field or timestamp citations
- **Affected participants or locations** — included only when authorized
- **Hypotheses** — possible causes clearly labelled as hypotheses, not conclusions
- **Recommended checks** — actionable next steps for responders
- **Confidence** — assessment of evidence strength and coverage
- **Data gaps** — missing telemetry, unavailable fields, or incomplete time ranges
